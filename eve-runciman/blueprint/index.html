Lead tracking built in

Every conversion

Buyer alert signup, Sell vs Rent report, private list and contact each fire a separate tracked lead event.

Seller intent

The report records whether the owner chose Selling or Renting, so follow-up starts in the right place.

AI source

Visits from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Copilot and others are tagged and stored on the lead.

Consent

Each lead stores how the person agreed to be contacted: email, WhatsApp or both.

Contact clicks

WhatsApp, phone and email clicks are tracked on every page.

Search behavior

Property filters show which neighborhoods and price points buyers ask for.

Every public page, with private and confirmation pages left out.

Structured data

RealEstateAgent, Person, FAQPage, Article and BreadcrumbList schema that tie you to your areas and services.

Answer-first pages

Each guide opens with a direct answer, carries your name, and covers Florida rules.

robots.txt and llms.txt make the site easy to read, and they are cheap to include. No major AI company has confirmed it uses llms.txt, so the plan below puts its effort into what AI tools do quote: clear pages under your name, and trusted sites that mention you. These files take effect at your domain root at launch. This mock-up stays hidden from search engines and AI crawlers.

Decisions needed from you

#DecisionWhy it matters
1Exact licensed brokerage name and your license typeIt appears on every page and in the schema
2Website domainAll canonical links, schema and llms.txt point to it. reaf.llc has no website today
3Report delivery: instant from a data feed, or prepared by you?The layout shows the report, then says it is still being analyzed. Instant figures need a valuation and rent data source
4Which properties go into off-market alerts?NAR’s Clear Cooperation Policy counts email blasts as public marketing, so alert wording depends on your MLS rules
5WhatsApp number and accountAutomated alerts need the WhatsApp Business Platform with opted-in contacts and approved templates
6Investor and international pathYour brief names these audiences. Should the home page get a third card, and do you want Spanish or Portuguese versions?
7Real sample figuresA $450,000 pool home in South Miami and $1,800 net from $2,500 rent will read as unrealistic to local buyers and owners
8ProofClient reviews, closed transactions and press are missing from the layout and help both trust and AI citations
