Common Questions

Who is Eve Runciman?

Eve Runciman is a Miami-based real estate professional and Florida-licensed attorney with over 20 years of experience in real estate, business transactions and law, working with local, out-of-state and international clients.

What is a Sell vs. Rent Report?

A free comparison for one Miami property. It sets the estimated market value and net proceeds from selling against the estimated monthly rent and net rental income, with a market outlook for each. Read the guide.

What are off-market properties?

Homes offered privately rather than listed on the MLS. Availability is never guaranteed, so buyers register their criteria and receive an alert when a match appears.

Is there any obligation?

No. Alerts and reports are free. Registering does not create a buyer representation agreement, and there is no obligation to buy, list or sell.
