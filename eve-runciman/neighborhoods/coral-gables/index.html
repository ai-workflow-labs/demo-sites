Coral Gables Real Estate Guide

By Eve Runciman. Updated November 2026. Draft for Eve’s review.

Short answerCoral Gables is a planned city just southwest of downtown Miami, known for Mediterranean Revival architecture, the Biltmore Hotel, Miracle Mile and the University of Miami. Exterior work on homes here is subject to architectural review, so check it before you buy to renovate.

Property in Coral Gables

Developed by George Merrick in the 1920s, the city has historic Mediterranean Revival homes, newer single-family houses, waterfront estates and condos near its downtown.

Before you buy

  • Check whether the home is historically designated.
  • Ask how the city’s Board of Architects applies to any changes you plan.
  • Get insurance quotes early, especially for waterfront property and older homes.
