Buying Miami Property from Another State or Country

By Eve Runciman, Florida-licensed attorney and real estate professional. Updated November 2026. Draft for Eve’s review.

Short answerOut-of-state and many foreign buyers can purchase property in Miami. Plan your financing, insurance and closing early, and if you are a foreign buyer, confirm your eligibility under Florida law before you make an offer.

Check these before you make an offer

Eligibility

Chapter 692, Florida Statutes, restricts purchases by certain foreign principals connected to designated countries of concern. Buyers sign an affidavit at closing. The rules depend on your status and on the property’s location, so check early.

Financing

Cash buyers usually move fastest. Foreign national loan programs exist, and terms vary by lender.

Insurance

Get windstorm and flood quotes before you commit, especially for waterfront property.

Property taxes

Homestead benefits apply only to your permanent Florida residence. A second home or rental property does not qualify.

Rental plans

Condo documents and local rules can limit rentals, especially short-term ones. Confirm them before you rely on rental income.

Closing from a distance

Florida allows remote online notarization, and a power of attorney is another route. Always confirm wire instructions by phone, using a number you already trust.

When you sell later

Under FIRPTA, a buyer generally must withhold 15% of the sale price when the seller is a foreign person, subject to exceptions. Plan for this with a tax adviser before you buy.

Common questions

Can a foreign national buy property in Miami?

Many can. Chapter 692 restricts purchases by certain foreign principals, and buyers sign an affidavit at closing, so check eligibility before making an offer.

Can I close on a Miami property without traveling?

Often, yes. Ask your closing agent whether they accept remote online notarization or a power of attorney.

What tax applies when a foreign owner sells?

A buyer generally must withhold 15% of the sale price under FIRPTA, subject to exceptions.
